## Key Decisions

### Completed, Not Pursuing (March 2026)

**Decision:** Stop building features for TalentAgent. Use as portfolio piece. Redirect energy to personal portfolio AI features (react-portfolio / luislozoya.com) and job searching.

**Why:**
- Market is crowded with funded competitors (Teal, Jobscan, Careerflow, Huntr, Simplify)
- "Paste a JD, get a score" is commoditized — ChatGPT does it for free
- Solo dev can't compete on feature breadth against funded teams
- Job seekers churn when they land a role — constant acquisition problem
- The Nate B Jones approach works better on a personal portfolio than as a SaaS

**What's differentiated (if revisited):**
- Ghost job detection
- Honest "don't apply" signals
- Bilingual EN/ES market potential
